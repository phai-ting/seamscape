1Screens

Check your monitors, then set each one's diagonal from its spec sheet.

What are these numbers?

Resolution is the monitor's native resolution. Position is where it sits in your OS display settings, in native pixels, with the primary monitor at 0, 0. Monitors left of or above the primary have negative values. In Chrome and Edge, "Detect all screens" fills these in for you.

2Gaps

Measure them from one phone photo of your desk, or with a ruler.

With your phone

  1. Check every monitor's diagonal in step 1. The photo measures only the gaps and offsets between them.
  2. Show the calibration screens, scan the QR code with your phone, and take one photo from where you sit.
  3. Type the code your phone shows, or open the link it sends you.

With a ruler

Measure from the edge of the lit picture on one screen to the edge of the lit picture on the next: both bezels plus any space between. Millimetres; 1 inch = 25.4 mm.

3Picture

Drag to move the picture, scroll to zoom, or drop a picture on the sheet. The faint picture outside each screen is the part your bezels and gaps will hide.
