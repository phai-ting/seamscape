Seamscape

Wallpaper that lines up across your monitors, as if you were looking through a window.

1 Your screens

Seamscape reads your monitor setup from the browser. Check the list, then set each monitor's size (the diagonal from its spec sheet).

# Primary Resolution (px) Position on desktop (px) Diagonal (in) Picture size Remove
What are these numbers?

Resolution is the monitor's native resolution. Position is where the monitor sits in your OS display settings, in native pixels, with the primary monitor at 0, 0. Monitors to the left of or above the primary have negative values. On Chrome and Edge, "Detect all screens" fills these in for you.

2 Gaps between screens

With a ruler, measure from the edge of the lit picture on one screen to the edge of the lit picture on the next. This includes both bezels and any space between the monitors. All values are in millimetres (1 inch = 25.4 mm).

3 Picture

Drag to move the picture, scroll to zoom. Dimmed areas fall behind bezels and gaps and won't be shown. You can also drop a picture here.
