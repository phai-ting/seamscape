Start on your computer

Open seamscape.phaiting.com on the computer with your monitors. Under Gaps, choose “Show calibration screens”, then scan the QR code it shows with this phone.

1Photograph your screens

    • Sit where you usually sit, so the photo sees what you see.
    • Fit every screen in the frame, with a little room around the edges.
    • Use the main camera (1×), not the wide lens.
    • Turn off lamps that reflect in the screens.
